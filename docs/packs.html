Packs & Shops

Emeralds buy card packs, and packs are where most new cards come from.

The Shop Row

Drop emeralds or Emerald Blocks on a pack in your shop row to buy it, then right-click it to open. Right-click a shop pack to see every card it can give. Each pack's first card is always its kind, and packs reroll ideas you already know. Quests and purchases unlock more packs.

The Nether Shop Row

The Nether shop row takes Gold Ingots, and each pack unlocks the next. The Wastes Pack is always open. The Fortress Pack opens after A Fortress in the Fire or 3 Wastes Packs, and the Bastion Pack after The Bastion or 3 Fortress Packs.

                  Wandering Trader

                    Piglin Merchant

                    Now and then a Piglin Merchant visits your Nether table by day, with one rare card for 8 gold.

                    Selling

                    Drop cards on the Sell box at the end of your shop row, or on a Market, to sell them. A card's tooltip shows what it's worth. Whatever's left over after whole emeralds pays out in Emerald Shards, a quarter of an emerald each, and 4 Shards stack into an Emerald. Diamonds are for crafting and can't be sold.

                    Emeralds and books don't take up card slots, so selling is also how you make room. See Card Slots.

                    Trading Post

                    A Trading Post swaps 9 emeralds for an Emerald Block and back, and shops count an Emerald Block as 9 emeralds. It sells packs too, and it posts an Order every few days: fill it within 2 days for emeralds.

                    Wandering Carpenter

                    After your third supper a Wandering Carpenter starts coming by. He trades Table Leaves for emeralds, and each Table Leaf makes your side table bigger. See Table Size.
