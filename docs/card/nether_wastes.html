Nether Wastes

Nether Wastes

Put a villager on it to gather Netherrack, Quartz, Gold Nuggets and Glowstone. Sometimes leads to other biomes.

What It Gives

With a Villager

Every 10s, one of these:

  • Netherrack40%
  • Nether Quartz22%
  • Gold Nugget20%
  • Glowstone Dust8%
  • Crimson Forest3%
  • Soul Sand Valley3%
  • Basalt Deltas2%
  • Warped Forest2%

With Diamond Pickaxe

Every 14s, one of these:

  • Netherrack30%
  • Gold Nugget25%
  • Nether Quartz25%
  • Glowstone Dust10%
  • Ancient Debris6%
  • Zombified Piglin4%

With Netherite Pickaxe

Every 12s, one of these:

  • Netherrack26%
  • Nether Quartz25%
  • Gold Nugget23%
  • Ancient Debris12%
  • Glowstone Dust10%
  • Zombified Piglin4%

With Compass

Every 30s, one of these:

  • Basalt Deltas25%
  • Crimson Forest25%
  • Soul Sand Valley25%
  • Warped Forest25%
