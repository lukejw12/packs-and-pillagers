The Nether

Chapter 2 opens a second table in the Nether, with its own places, monsters, shops, and bosses.

Getting There

Build a Nether Portal from 10 Obsidian and Flint and Steel, or repair a Ruined Portal. Right-click the portal to travel to your Nether table, and stack cards on it to send them across.

Nether Places

Work the Nether Wastes for gold. A Villager with a Compass on the Wastes finds a place you haven't seen yet, and the first trip to each one brings its key cards.

Food Goes Bad

Food left on the Nether table chars after 4 days and is worth half, then burns to Ash 4 days later. Ice next to Nether food halves charring.

Piglins and Gold

Piglins attack villagers without gold. Drop a Gold Ingot on one to barter for a random card, or give it 3 at once to recruit it as an Allied Piglin. A Golden Helmet keeps Piglins off its wearer, and a Gilded Charm calms every Piglin on the table.

Nether Shops

The Nether shop row takes Gold Ingots, and each pack unlocks the next. The Wastes Pack is always open. The Fortress Pack opens after A Fortress in the Fire or 3 Wastes Packs, and the Bastion Pack after The Bastion or 3 Fortress Packs. Now and then a Piglin Merchant visits by day with one rare card for 8 gold. See Packs & Shops.

The Lava Sea

Put a Saddle on a Strider. With a Villager and a Warped Fungus on a Stick, the Saddled Strider finds the Lava Sea, then carries villagers out to work it for gold, Ancient Debris, and treasure.

Nether Nights

Some Nether nights bring a Ghast Storm or a Hoglin Stampede, and Hoglins taken through the portal turn into Zoglins.

Fortress and Bastion

Nether Wastes trips can turn up a Nether Fortress gateway, and the 10th trip always does. Crimson Forest trips do the same for the Bastion Remnant. The Fortress ends with the Wildfire, whose shields break fastest to ranged attacks. The Bastion ends with the Mutated Hoglin. See Gateways & Bosses.

Netherite

Ancient Debris from the Lava Sea turns into Netherite, and a Smithing Table upgrades your diamond gear.

The Wither and the Beacon

Put 3 Wither Skeleton Skulls on 4 Soul Sand to summon the Wither. While it flies, only ranged fighters can hurt it. Its Nether Star builds a Beacon: feed it minerals for a buff on your table.

Helpers

Ender Chests share one storage across both tables. A Respawn Anchor charged with Glowstone Dust brings back a villager who falls on the Nether table, but it explodes on the overworld table. The Barter Station, Nether Wart Farm, and Blaze Spawner work on their own.

Nether Automation

The Wrench works on your Nether table too. Pipe cards into a Nether Portal and they come out of the portal on your other table. Nether nodes have output ports like the overworld ones. A Brewing Stand brews on its own from piped ingredients, and Blaze Powder makes it brew two at once.

Chapter 2 Quests

The main quest line carries on into the Nether. See Quests for every step.
