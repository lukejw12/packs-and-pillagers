Out in the World

Your table and the real world feed each other.

New Lands

Visiting the Badlands, Snowy Plains, or a Swamp in your world puts its card on your table. The Badlands give gold and Terracotta, the Snowy Plains give Ice, which halves charring next to Nether food, and the Swamp gives Slimeballs for Magma Cream. Witches come at night once you know a Swamp.

Bringing Mobs In

Right-click a villager or animal with the Card Board to bring it onto your table.

Links to Your World

Sneak and right-click a real barrel with your Card Board to link it, and a Linked Barrel card appears on your table. Right-click the card to switch between pulling items in and sending piped cards out to that barrel. Place a real Lodestone anywhere, even in the Nether, and that spot becomes a waystone on your table.

Sending Cards Home

Drop cards on a Bundle to send them to your real inventory. Most resources and gear turn into the real item, and enchanted gear comes out with its enchantment. Villagers arrive as real villagers with their trades. Enchanted Books, potions, and maps become the real thing, and talismans turn into charms that work in your off hand.

Maps and Structures

A Villager with a Compass can draw a map to a structure. Send it home with a Bundle for a real explorer map, then find the structure to open its gateway on your table. A Villager holding an explorer map opens that map's gateway too. See Gateways & Bosses.

Waystones and Graves

Craft a Waystone Kit, send it home, and right-click where you want a waystone. It shows up on your table too, so you can right-click it there to leave the table at that spot.

With a Shrine on your table, the things you drop when you die land on it as a Grave. Right-click the Grave to get your things back when you leave the table.
