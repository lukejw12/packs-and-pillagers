Gateways & Bosses

Real places open gateways on your table. Stack villagers on one and right-click to send them through. They fight a few waves and then a boss, healing a little between waves. Win, and the loot comes home as one stack. Gateways from your world fade after 3 days.

A Villager with an explorer map opens that map's gateway on your table. Some rare finds keep count: the action bar shows how many trips are left until one is guaranteed.

Stable Portals

Win a run through the Trial, Sculk, Ocean, or Mansion gateway and you learn its Stable Portal, so you can go back whenever you like. Build one from a Villager, 4 Cobblestone, a Diamond, and the place's key. Each run through it costs another key.

Redstone Caves

The Redstone Caves are a wave area: five waves of Redstone Cubes, with Redstone Golems as minibosses. Stack villagers on the card and right-click to send them in. The card never fades, so go back as often as you like. Golems sometimes drop a Charged Redstone Core. Clearing them can give:

  • Emerald
  • Iron Ingot
  • Copper Ingot
  • Gold Ingot
  • Golden Apple
  • Diamond

The Redstone Monstrosity

Stack a Charged Redstone Core with a Summoning Orb. Evokers sometimes drop the orb. After 30 seconds the Redstone Monstrosity bursts onto your table. It slams the ground, calls Redstone Cubes, and is shielded while any of them are alive. Beat it with your villagers to finish Chapter 1.

The Wither

Put 3 Wither Skeleton Skulls on 4 Soul Sand to summon the Wither. While it flies, only ranged fighters can hurt it. Its Nether Star builds a Beacon: feed it minerals for a buff on your table. See The Nether.
